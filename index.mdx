Orbit is a deployment and observability platform for engineering teams. Connect a repository, and Orbit builds and deploys every push, routes traffic to your domains, streams structured logs, runs synthetic monitors from multiple regions, and pages the right people when something breaks.

## What Orbit does

## How it fits together

An **organization** owns everything: projects, members, API keys, webhooks, and billing. Inside it:

- A **project** maps to one Git repository and has its own environment variables, domains, and deployment history.
- A **deployment** is one immutable build of a commit. Production deployments serve your primary domain; preview deployments get a unique URL per branch.
- **Monitors** and **incidents** live at the organization level so a single on-call rotation can cover every service.
- **Feature flags** are evaluated at the edge and can be flipped without redeploying.
