An incident is a record of something being wrong, who is handling it, and what has been done. Incidents are created automatically by monitors and failed production deployments, or manually from a log line or the Incidents page.

## Severity

| Severity | Use when | Default notification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Production is down or data is at risk | Page on-call immediately |
| **High** | Major feature degraded for many users | Slack + page after 10 minutes unacknowledged |
| **Medium** | Partial degradation or elevated errors | Slack |
| **Low** | Cosmetic, or an automated warning such as a failed certificate renewal | Email digest |

## Lifecycle

```
Open ──▶ Acknowledged ──▶ Resolved
                 ▲                │
                 └──── Reopen ────┘
```

- **Open** — created but nobody has claimed it. The sidebar badge and header bell count these.
- **Acknowledged** — someone is on it. Acknowledging stops escalation.
- **Resolved** — fixed. Resolving notifies subscribers and closes any linked monitor alert.

## The Incidents page

The left column lists incidents filtered to **Active** (open + acknowledged), **Resolved**, or **All**. Select one to see its detail on the right: severity and status badges, the affected service, start and resolve times, an assignee picker, and the timeline.

## Timeline

Every status change, assignment, and posted update appears in the timeline with its author and time. Use the text box at the bottom to post an update; keep updates short and factual, and include deployment IDs so readers can jump to the [build](/deployments/overview) involved.

## Linking

Incident IDs (`inc_204`) are referenced from:

- The project **Overview** alert banner when a project is **Down**
- Notification bell entries and `?focus=<id>` deep links
- [Webhook](/organization/webhooks) events `incident.opened` and `incident.resolved`
- The [audit log](/organization/audit-log) as `incident.resolve`

## Postmortems

After resolving a **Critical** or **High** incident, Orbit prompts for a postmortem on the timeline. Postmortems are optional but are exported with the incident in the monthly reliability report.
