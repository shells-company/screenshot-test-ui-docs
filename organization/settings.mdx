Settings are grouped into five tabs. Link directly to one with `?tab=<name>`, for example `/settings?tab=billing`.

## General

Organization name, URL slug, default region for new projects, and the timezone used for dates in the dashboard and digests. The **Danger zone** holds **Delete organization**, which requires the owner to confirm by email; all data is purged after 30 days.

## Billing

- **Current plan** with the option to upgrade, downgrade, or contact sales for Enterprise. Plan changes take effect immediately and are prorated.
- **Payment method** managed through the Stripe billing portal.
- **Invoices** for download as PDF.

## Notifications

Per-member email preferences (deployment failures, incident updates, weekly digest, usage alerts, product updates) and organization-wide **Slack routing**. Expand a routing rule to change its channel and message template; templates support `{{event}}`, `{{project}}`, `{{actor}}`, and `{{url}}`.

## Security

- **Require two-factor authentication** with a 14-day grace period for existing members.
- **SAML single sign-on** status and identity provider.
- **Session duration** — 1, 7, or 30 days.
- **IP allowlist** — CIDR ranges allowed to reach the dashboard and API. Your current IP is shown so you don't lock yourself out.

Every change on this page is written to the [audit log](/organization/audit-log) as `settings.update`.
