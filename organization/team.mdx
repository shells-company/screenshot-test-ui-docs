The **Team** page lists every member of the organization with their role, status, two-factor state, join date, and last activity. The header shows seats used against the plan's seat count.

## Inviting members

1. Click **Invite members**.
2. Enter one or more email addresses, separated by commas or new lines.
3. Choose a role. The role applies to everyone in this batch; you can change individuals afterward.
4. Click **Send invites**.

Invitees appear immediately with the **Invited** status and count toward seats. Invitations expire after 7 days; use **Resend invite** in the row menu to send a fresh one.

## Changing roles

Use the role dropdown inline in the table. The change takes effect on the member's next request and is recorded in the [audit log](/organization/audit-log) as `member.role_change`. The organization owner's role can't be changed here; ownership is transferred from [Settings → General](/organization/settings).

See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## Two-factor authentication

The shield icon shows whether a member has 2FA enabled. When **Require two-factor authentication** is on in [Settings → Security](/organization/settings#security), members without it have a 14-day grace period, after which they are locked out until they enroll. Hover the amber shield to see how much of the grace period remains.

## Suspending and removing

From the row menu:

- **Sign out everywhere** revokes all of the member's sessions and API keys they created. Use it first if you suspect a compromised account.
- **Remove from organization** deletes their membership. Resources they created (projects, flags, keys) remain and are attributed to them in history.

Contractors whose engagement has ended are usually **suspended** rather than removed so their history stays intact. Suspended members can't sign in but still appear in the list and in audit entries.

## Filtering

Search matches names and emails. The role filter narrows to one role, which is useful for auditing who has **Admin** access.
